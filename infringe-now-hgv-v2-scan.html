Rule library

Driving limits

  • Daily driving limit is generally 9 hours, extendable to 10 hours no more than twice in a week.
  • Weekly driving limit is generally 56 hours.
  • Two-week driving limit is generally 90 hours.
  • After 4.5 hours of driving, a qualifying break is normally required.

Important

Rules can vary by journey, vehicle and applicable regime. The library is provided for guidance and should be checked against current official rules.
