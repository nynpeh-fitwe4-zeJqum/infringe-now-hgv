Compliance check

Designed around the UK assimilated drivers' hours rules for goods vehicles. If your journey is under a different regime or an exemption applies, use the result as a prompt to check the correct rules.

Result

Infringement library

Daily drivingNormally maximum 9 hours. It can be extended to 10 hours no more than twice in a fixed week.
Weekly drivingMaximum 56 hours in a fixed week.
Two-week drivingMaximum 90 hours over any 2 consecutive weeks.
BreakAfter no more than 4.5 hours of driving, take at least 45 minutes. A qualifying split is 15 minutes followed by 30 minutes.
Daily restRegular daily rest is at least 11 continuous hours. It can be reduced to at least 9 hours up to 3 times between weekly rests.
Weekly restA regular weekly rest is at least 45 hours. A reduced weekly rest is at least 24 hours and less than 45 hours, subject to the compensation rules.
Weekly-rest timingA weekly rest must normally start no later than the end of six consecutive 24-hour periods from the end of the previous weekly rest.

Important

The app does not decide whether an offence has legally occurred. The complete tachograph record, manual entries, applicable exemptions, route, vehicle and other facts may change the assessment.
