Infringement library

4.5-hour driving break

Under assimilated/AETR rules, a break of at least 45 minutes is required after no more than 4.5 hours driving. It may be split into 15 minutes plus 30 minutes.

Daily driving

Maximum 9 hours, extendable to 10 hours no more than twice in a week.

Weekly driving

Maximum 56 hours.

Two-week driving

Maximum 90 hours across any two consecutive weeks.

Daily rest

Normally at least 11 hours. It can be reduced to 9 hours up to 3 times between weekly rest periods.

Weekly rest

A regular weekly rest is at least 45 hours. A reduced weekly rest is at least 24 hours and less than 45 hours, with compensation rules.

GB domestic goods rules

Where the GB domestic rules apply, daily driving is normally limited to 10 hours and daily duty to 11 hours. Break and rest requirements are different from assimilated rules.

Working time

For mobile workers under the assimilated/AETR regime, working time averages 48 hours per week, with up to 60 hours in a single week provided the average is maintained. Night work has additional limits.
