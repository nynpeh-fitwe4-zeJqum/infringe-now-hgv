Key rules reference
Quick guide

  

    
Daily drivingNormally up to 9 hours, with permitted extensions to 10 hours on limited days.

    
Weekly drivingMaximum 56 hours in a week.

    
Two-week totalMaximum 90 hours across two consecutive weeks.

    
4.5-hour ruleA driver must take the required break after no more than 4.5 hours of driving.

    
Daily restDaily rest has specific requirements and permitted reductions. Check the full rules for the circumstances.

    
Weekly restRegular and reduced weekly rest have specific requirements and exceptions.

  






  
About
Infringe Now HGV

  

    
Built by Kevin Watts

    
Infringe Now HGV is a driver-focused prototype designed to make everyday compliance checks quicker and easier on a phone.

    
This prototype is a screening aid, not a legal determination. Always verify results against the current official rules and the circumstances of the journey.
