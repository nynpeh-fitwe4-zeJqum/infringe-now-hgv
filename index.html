Key rules reference
Quick guide

 

  
Daily drivingNormally up to 9 hours, with permitted extensions to 10 hours on limited days.

  
Weekly drivingMaximum 56 hours in a week.

  
Two-week totalMaximum 90 hours across two consecutive weeks.

  
Continuous drivingA break is required after no more than 4.5 hours of driving.

About Infringe Now HGV
Built for drivers

 

  
Developed by Kevin Watts

  
Infringe Now HGV started as a practical idea from the HGV driving world: make compliance information easier to access, understand and check on a phone.

  
Current version: working prototype. The next stages are real-driver testing, improved printout scanning, secure accounts and a full mobile-app experience.
